Our Story

Where Every
Cup Tells a Story

Casa Lalo was born from a simple dream: to create a space where East Harlem’s vibrant community could gather over great coffee, bold flavors, and good company.

Nestled on Lexington Avenue between 100th and 101st streets, our intimate 22-seat café bridges the warmth of a corner coffee shop with the soul of a Mexican kitchen. From morning chilaquiles to evening enchiladas, every dish is crafted with care, using recipes inspired by generations of tradition.

We believe in the Mexican concept of the “third place”—not home, not work, but that essential gathering spot where neighbors become friends and strangers become regulars. That’s Casa Lalo.
