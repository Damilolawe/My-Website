WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGES / 001

Less template.
More identity.

A cast member doesn't need a corporate dashboard. A developer shouldn't look like a florist. And a small business deserves more than a generic hero, three identical cards and a contact form.

We start with what the site needs to *do*, then create a visual language to match. The examples below are fictional demonstrations of that philosophy, not claims of completed client projects.
