## Editorial exemplars
- **Folio No. 01:** Offset print folios; large italics, biography at magazine measure, service spreads and dark contact closing.
- **Signal / Noise:** Graphic broadcast; loud typographic contact, staggered two-column service posters, boxed biography, non-overflowing tape.
- **Ledger & Co.:** Commercial brief; grid-based service offering and numbered tables, structured biography and confident close.

## Original references and design principles
- A24: project-led dramatic hierarchy; reinterpret as After Hours cinematic chapters rather than borrowing creative.
- Active Theory: expressive movement; reinterpret as motion appropriate to each template, without runtime/3D requirement.
- Stripe Press: reading measure and publication scale; reinterpret as Folio's issue-like typography.
- NTS: live editorial content density; reinterpret as Signal's poster broadcast grid.
- MoMA: discoverable objects and supporting labels; reinterpret as Museum Notes' exhibit reading hierarchy.
- Studio Freight: brand idea determines composition; reinterpret as unique template interior architectures.
- Gufram: playful but consistent object language; reinterpret as Daylight and Bloom's shaped papers, not borrowed assets.
