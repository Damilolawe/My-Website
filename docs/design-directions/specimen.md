## Concept

Let every word perform. Build a responsive, content-first customer site organized around **type atlas**, with a recognizably different first fold from the other 39 designs. Treat the hero as a designed composition, not a decorated generic card. Use the existing site client's actual content, uploaded imagery, and selected website purpose.

## Visual priorities

- **Hierarchy:** Strong hero focal point, readable intro text, quiet utility labels and editorial section headings.
- **Accent:** `#35302d` is the initial user-editable primary accent, not a mandatory permanent brand color.
- **Art direction:** Original geometry and typographic ornament inspired by the idea of type atlas; never show reference-site logos or screenshots.
- **Rhythm:** Use variable chapter heights, custom framing, and asymmetric project groupings. The portfolio and services sections should visually follow the chosen architecture.
- **Motion:** CSS-only, short, purposeful reveals and hover response; honor reduced-motion. No auto-scroll and no animation-off widget.

## Content and implementation contract

- Public demos are conspicuously fictional, with no invented testimonials, events, sales, credentials or working external links.
- Customer pages render their actual name, headline, bio, structured projects, verified links, permitted photos, services, testimonials and contact method, respecting section order and visibility.
- All user-provided strings are escaped; only validated HTTPS links become external anchors. No external images, fonts, scripts or cookies ship in the generated output.
- Design selectors must support primary and secondary color changes, mobile preview and client content without breaking the architecture.
- Test at 320px, 375px, 768px and desktop; verify color contrast, long names, safe links, keyboard navigation and no horizontal overflow.
