A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING
Good questions.
Clear answers.
I’m a student. Is this for me?
Absolutely. A portfolio, side project, creative practice, or first business is a great starting point. Tell me what you need and the budget you have in mind so we can discuss a sensible scope.
What does a website cost?
One-page websites start at $75 and multi-page websites at $150. Premium / AWE Custom projects receive a personalized quote after we review your brief. Final pricing depends on scope. Domain, hosting, and ongoing services are quoted separately when needed.
Can you build more than a portfolio?
Yes. Explore the business concept and Orbit demo to try interactive menus, task management, filtering, and data exports. More complex products need their own discussion about integrations, data, and ongoing operation.
Are these all client projects?
The Spotlight, Slow Hours, and Orbit are fictional design concepts created to demonstrate capabilities. ResidenceOS and Cirvo are original products in development. No fictional concept is presented as paid client work.
What do I need to get started?
A rough goal is enough for a first conversation. Helpful extras include content, images you have permission to use, a few websites you like, and an ideal launch date. Use the brief builder to put it together.
