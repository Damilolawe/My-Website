CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Your story stays yours.

Project inquiries

If you use the public contact form, Netlify Forms collects your name, email, project category, and message so AWE / DIGITAL can review and respond. A successful form submission is not a contract or paid order. Avoid submitting sensitive information. Contact submissions are stored by Netlify according to its service settings; deletion or privacy requests can be directed to the business contact once published.

What this platform processes

When accounts and ordering are available, AWE / DIGITAL processes your sign-in email, order selections, supplied content, uploaded files, payment status, and revision requests to prepare and deliver your website. Don’t upload information you do not have permission to share.

Private materials and public content

Résumés and original documents are stored privately. They are not automatically extracted or published. Selected photos and information marked for your website become public only after owner approval. Do not include residential addresses or private phone numbers in public content fields. Image uploads are resized and re-encoded to remove embedded metadata.

Providers

The configured service uses Netlify for hosting and server functions, Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage, Stripe for hosted checkout, and Resend for delivery emails. Card information goes directly to Stripe, not this website’s database. Routine template generation does not use third-party AI services. Optional fonts on the studio homepage load from Google Fonts.

Your account

An essential, secure session cookie supports sign-in for up to one hour. Signing out clears it. No marketing analytics or advertising cookies are configured. The homepage remembers only your motion preference.

Hosting, ownership and revisions

These are defined in the package you review before checkout. Do not assume unlimited hosting, a free custom domain, unlimited changes, or an ownership transfer. Unpublished revision drafts do not replace the current live version until approved.

Launch status and privacy requests

Live payments are disabled. Before accepting real orders, the studio must publish its support contact and retention/deletion policy and verify the complete service. Use your signed-in project brief or revision request to contact the studio once accounts are enabled.
