03 / The ritual

The art of slowing down.

The tools, the tea and the care between them are part of every cup.

Hand-whisked to order

Every detail has a purpose.

Japanese matcha is carefully prepared by combining finely powdered green tea with water and whisking it into a smooth drink. Jane's Tearoom places that process at the center of the experience.

Alongside matcha, the menu was designed for leisurely loose-leaf tea service. A shared pot of hojicha, genmaicha or sencha invites people to stay, talk and discover differences between each tea.
