01 / From the Heights, with love

A home for the tea community.

What began at a farmers market now has a permanent place at 93 Franklin Street.

Founder / Jane Yang

From pop-up to place.

Jane's Matcha began as a pop-up in 2024, becoming a familiar sight at Jersey City markets and later at The Alps. Founder Jane Yang's love of Japanese tea shaped each hand-whisked drink and the community that formed around the business.

After time spent learning about tea culture in Japan, Yang brought that inspiration into the first permanent tearoom, which soft-opened on Franklin Street in July 2026. The neighborhood support that began the journey remains at its center.
