Who Is

Tahir Moore

From East St. Louis to the Los Angeles stage — Tahir Moore moved out west in 2009 to chase stand-up full time, and he has been building ever since.

His comedy carried straight to the screen, with credits on Abbott Elementary, Insecure and A Workplace Comedy. On the mic and in front of the camera, Tahir keeps the storytelling sharp and the laughs honest.

Off the stage he is a podcast machine, creating podcasts and original series and bringing the same energy to every feed he touches.
