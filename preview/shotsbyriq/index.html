01 / RESCHEDULING

THE CHANGE OF PLANS.

Rescheduled shoots require a $50 fee. Same-day rescheduling requires $100.

02 / CANCELLATIONS

THE 48-HOUR RULE.

Give at least 48 hours’ notice. Later cancellations incur a fee equal to the remaining balance of the cancelled service.

03 / IMAGE SELECTION

YOUR SEVEN DAYS.

Choose and approve the images you want edited within 7 days. After that, the booking policy says edits are no longer eligible.

04 / DELIVERY

GOOD WORK TAKES TIME.

Standard edited-image turnaround is 3–14 days after your selected images have been approved.

05 / AFTER DELIVERY

THE FINAL LOOK.

Request corrections within 24 hours of receiving edits. Additional edits after that window may incur a fee.

06 / ON SET

COME CAMERA-READY.

Hair and makeup artists are welcome, but arrive with styling completed. Contact the photographer first if you need on-site preparation; fees may apply.

STRICT NO-REFUND POLICY
