08 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
get started.

Clear answers to the questions most entrepreneurs ask first.

Is business advising really free?

Yes. Pace SBDC provides confidential, no-cost business counseling, training, and research to entrepreneurs and small business owners.

Do I need to already own a business?

No. Aspiring entrepreneurs with a specific venture in mind can also seek advising.

Can I meet with someone virtually?

Yes. The center offers remote sessions by phone or video, as well as in-person options.

Can I receive assistance in Spanish?

Yes. Request Spanish-language counseling in your initial inquiry.

How do I request an appointment?

Submit the official NYSBDC eCenter Direct intake form. A team member will contact you to coordinate a session.
