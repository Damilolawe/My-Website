# Awe Concepts preview design and imagery rules

These rules apply to existing and future website concepts under `preview/`.

1. **Never use a competitor's photographs, logos, promotional images, website screenshots, or social media photos as another business's imagery.** This includes other restaurants that serve similar food. Photographs must not misleadingly portray a different business as the prospect.
2. Prefer verified imagery supplied by or belonging to the target business and retain its established color palette and logo proportions. If authentic imagery cannot be sourced and used appropriately, substitute **high-resolution, relevant, commercially usable, watermark-free stock photography** (such as free-licensed Unsplash or Pexels photos), not a competitor's images. Check the actual license, image subject, quality, source URL, and suitability before use.
3. For pre-opening businesses and stock examples, retain a discreet, site-wide independent design-concept notice. Do not imply that representative photos depict the business, its dishes or completed interiors. Do not overlay each image with stock-photo captions; avoid unverified menu claims.
4. Replace visible placeholders or clip-art stand-ins with editorial photography when a suitable relevant image is available. Do not use text-over-face cropping; verify object positioning and responsive image layout.
5. Every homepage motion/ticker banner must loop continuously without pauses, jank, blanks, or a seam at **desktop and mobile widths**. Duplicate equal-width motion units; test at 1440px, 1024px, 768px and 390px widths.
6. Check logo kerning, symbol spacing, line wrapping, navigation and buttons at desktop and mobile breakpoints. Respect native brand colors and preserve the existing **Designed by Awe Concepts** footer credit and production/main-site separation.
7. Validate all photo URLs, anchor links, imagery licensing, animation, HTML structure and site-specific test suites before shipping. Ensure GitHub preview deployment succeeds and verify the live rendering. Never claim visual verification if it was not performed.
