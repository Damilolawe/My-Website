On board

A pizza place, first.

Battello's sister restaurant is built around pizza: crispy, artisan-style pies with fresh, seasonal toppings, plus the things you want around them. The plan is casual and approachable, with moderate prices.

  1. Pizzas

    Crispy, artisan-style, with seasonal toppings. The center of the menu.

  2. Paninis

    Pressed sandwiches for a lighter stop.

  3. Shared plates

    Appetizers and plates for the middle of the table.

  4. Salads

    Fresh greens to cut through the crust.

  5. Wine

    Pours to go with all of the above.

The full menu has not been published yet. Categories above come from the restaurant team's announced plans.

The crew

From the team next door.

Barchino comes from chef and partner Ryan DePersio, whose kitchens include Battello on the Newport marina, Ember & Eagle and The Kitchen Step. Where Battello is a waterfront dining room for contemporary Italian, Barchino is meant to be the easy, everyday stop beside it: a 120-seat pizza restaurant with room for events.
