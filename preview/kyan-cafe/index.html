On the avenue

A newer stop on 5th Avenue’s coffee walk.

The Bay Ridge 5th Avenue BID announced Kyan Cafe’s arrival at 7603 5th Avenue in November 2025. In 2026 it was one of the stops on the BID’s Coffee Crawl, a tasting route along the avenue’s local cafes.
