GOOD TO KNOW / 07

QUESTIONS?
WE GOT YOU.

A few things to know before you jump in.

Is Otaku Haven free?

Yes. The core community experience is free. The app also offers an optional Premium tier with extra perks.

Can I use Otaku Haven on my phone?

Yes. The official app is available through Apple's App Store and Google Play. Use the download links below.

How do ranks and XP work?

Taking part in the community helps you earn experience points. As you engage, you can climb toward the highest rank, SSS.

What can I do inside the Haven?

Connect with fans through training bonds, debate in Shonen Showdown, share cosplay, explore creator storefronts, and take on quests with Zai.
