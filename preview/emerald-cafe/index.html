The family

Baked on Myrtle. Served on Saint Nicholas.

The Emerald Bakery & Cafe opened its main location at 1224 Myrtle Avenue, across from The Emerald Dispensary, with pastries and desserts baked in-house. The Emerald Cafe on Saint Nicholas Avenue brings the same welcome to a smaller room, with drinks and a limited run of grab-and-go from the bakery.
