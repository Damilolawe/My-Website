Good to know

What does Cultiva mean?

Cultiva is Spanish for “cultivate” — a good word for a cafe that is tended daily and grows with the block around it.

Is it good for working?

Yes — it is a calm neighborhood room made for a slow morning, a seat and a drink.

Do you have pastries?

A small case is baked through the morning, from croissants to a seasonal muffin.

How do I get there?

On the Queens side of Wyckoff Avenue, a short walk from the Halsey Street L station.
