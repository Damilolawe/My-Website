03 / A day at Isla

Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.

The day changes. The welcome stays.

Morning / Café

First things first: a flat white.

In Australia, coffee is part of the everyday conversation. Isla & Co brings that ritual to Brooklyn through carefully brewed espresso, house blends and a menu made for mornings.

From a quick catch-up to a longer brunch, the restaurant offers a flexible space to start the day. The neighborhood's energy comes into focus as coffee becomes lunch.

Evening / Cocktails

When the coffee becomes a martini.

The espresso martini flight, cocktail list and globally influenced dinner menu bring energy to later hours. The restaurant also advertises weekday and special-event promotions; the terms and current schedule should be confirmed on the official channels.

For events, group dining or special occasions, contact the venue directly to discuss the options rather than relying on invented capacity or packages.
