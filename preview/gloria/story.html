01 / The story

A new chapter on an old corner.

The team behind Glory Days brought a new neighborhood restaurant to Union Avenue in August 2026.

Union Avenue at Withers Street

A familiar corner. A fresh reason to meet.

Gloria opened on August 20, 2026, in the space formerly occupied by Reunion. The restaurant comes from the people behind nearby coffee shop Glory Days.

That connection to Williamsburg is the heart of this independent editorial concept: a table for the neighborhood, and a reason to get together at the end of the day.
