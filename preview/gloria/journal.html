A few verified details behind this Williamsburg opening—not invented news posts.

August 20, 2026

Gloria arrives.

Gloria debuted at 544 Union Avenue on August 20, 2026, taking over the former Reunion address. The founding team is also behind the nearby café Glory Days.

Its first public coverage described dinner service and a walk-in format. Because the concept is not operated by the restaurant, the current service schedule remains intentionally unlisted.
