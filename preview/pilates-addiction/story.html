The Aurum system

Control meets intensity.

Pilates Addiction pairs classical technique with modern, progressive training, guided by educators who emphasize form, connection and results.

Its patented Aurum™ apparatus combines a reformer, jump board, ballet barre and Wunda Chair. Four training formats offer different ways to challenge the body with low-impact movement.

This independent concept uses ordinary stock Pilates photography for atmosphere; the images do not depict the proprietary Aurum™ reformer.
