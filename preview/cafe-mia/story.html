01 / Our story

Built for the people around it.

A family project at the heart of Bushwick.

From the neighborhood

More than a morning coffee.

Arty and Dilyara created Café Mia as a family-run space in Bushwick, inspired by their daughter and their long connection to Brooklyn.

At 1128 Broadway, the café combines a carefully considered drinks program with a welcoming neighborhood environment.
