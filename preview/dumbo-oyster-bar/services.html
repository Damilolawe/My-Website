Oysters & chilled plates

The half-shell takes the spotlight.

Dumbo Oyster Bar's published menu lists Mystic, Bluepoint, Mintersweet, Beach Plum and Kumamoto oysters, with a rotating oyster selection and a tasting sampler. Chilled seafood and shrimp cocktails set the tone for the raw bar.

Beyond oysters, the menu includes lobster rolls, crab cakes, tuna tartare, grilled salmon and steak, with desserts such as Junior's cheesecake and tiramisu.
