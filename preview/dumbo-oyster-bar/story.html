01 / THE STORY

Where the city meets the sea.

An old-world hospitality team brings oysters, seafood and good company to the heart of DUMBO.

Opened 2026 / Brooklyn

A new reason to stay by the bridge.

Opened in 2026 at 7 Old Fulton Street, Dumbo Oyster Bar was created by hospitality veterans Gieto Nicaj and Kevin A. Mattner, with general manager Remy Kurtaj and executive chef Sabas Escudero helping shape the experience.

Nicaj and Mattner bring experience from the Greenwich Village institution One if by Land, Two if by Sea. In Brooklyn, the raw bar takes center stage with fresh oysters, seafood towers and warm service.
