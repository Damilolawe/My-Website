Ancestral hospitality, Greenpoint address

Kuttley House is a Yemeni coffee shop that soft opened at 216 Driggs Avenue on September 10, 2026. The team describes its roots in Yemen as the inspiration for the shop, and sets out to bring ancestral Yemeni hospitality to the neighborhood.

Yemen is one of the oldest names in coffee. The idea here is simple: make the cup, the pastry and the seat worth lingering over.
