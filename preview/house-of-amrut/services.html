02 / Inside the collection

For the curious palate.

A tasting experience built around rare Indian single malts and the people who share their stories.

Guided discoveries

A collection worth exploring.

The House of Amrut collection spans Amrut's Indian single malt whiskies, including rare and limited bottlings reported at its 2026 opening, such as Expedition and Greedy Angels. Representatives from Amrut have led guided tastings that introduce its history and whisky-making approach.

The cellar's bar also serves cocktails. Dishes from Brick Lane Curry House upstairs create Indian-inspired food pairings, with Chef Noor involved in the culinary program.

Current tasting flights, access, prices, bottle availability and special sessions require confirmation with the business. This independent study does not invent a booking schedule.
