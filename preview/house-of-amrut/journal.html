Jersey City / The cellar

Behind the quiet doorway.

House of Amrut is a speakeasy-style room below Brick Lane Curry House. Visitors enter by a discreet door and descend to an intimate cellar with table and bar seating.

Early coverage describes cocktails, Indian food pairings and live jazz on many evenings. Rather than presenting unconfirmed performers or dates, this concept links visitors to the business's own channel for current programming.
