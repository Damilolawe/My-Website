01 / The story

One man's fascination, a room of discovery.

Sati Sharma's passion for Indian single malt became a tasting room beneath downtown Jersey City.

The journey / 2012 to 2026

From a favorite pour to a new address.

Satinder “Sati” Sharma is a co-creator of Brick Lane Curry House, the British-style Indian restaurant group he helped launch in New York in 2002. Sharma became an Amrut whisky enthusiast in 2012.

A 2025 visit to the Amrut distillery opened his eyes to a much wider range of expressions. Over two years, he gathered rare bottles and shaped a way to introduce the collection to other whisky enthusiasts.

House of Amrut opened in October 2026 under Brick Lane Curry House at 136 Newark Avenue. Its intimate cellar setting lets the whisky and the people around the table take center stage.
