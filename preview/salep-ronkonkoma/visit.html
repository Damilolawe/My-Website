Good to know

Is the ice cream really made in house?

Yes — every flavor is churned on site in small batches, from the Turkish dondurma to the twenty-plus American-style flavors.

What is salep, exactly?

Salep is a fine flour milled from the bulbs of wild orchids. It is what gives Turkish ice cream its signature stretch and chew, and it is the base of the warm salep drink.

Do you do parties and catering?

Hand-packed pints and party tubs make it easy to take the shop home. Ask at the counter for larger orders.

Are there dairy-free options?

Italian ice and fruit sorbets are on hand for a lighter, dairy-free scoop.
