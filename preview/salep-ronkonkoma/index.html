What makes it stretch

Dondurma, done the traditional way.

Salep is thick, chewy and famously stretchy — thanks to salep, a fine powder milled from the bulbs of wild orchids, and a long, patient churn. It is scooped and served the old way, with a little showmanship at the counter.
