Good to know

Who is behind Tiny Topiary?

Founders Jason Kratochvil and Lynne Burke built it as a welcoming, inclusive neighborhood room in Bergen Hill.

Is it good for working or lingering?

Yes — it is designed as a community room for slow food and longer conversations.

Do you bake in house?

Mornings start with fresh bakery; the slow-food plates follow through the day.

Open on weekends?

Open seven days a week, with a gentler Sunday close.

See you in Bergen Hill.

Good food. Small wonder.
