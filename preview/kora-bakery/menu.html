The case

What's been behind the glass.

Kora's menu rotates with the seasons, so the case changes from week to week. These are bakes that have appeared on the menu and in press coverage of the Sunnyside shop. Ask at the counter what's out today.

Doughnuts

  • Leche Flan ni Lola

    The signature. A doughnut topped with leche flan from co-founder Kimberly Camara's late grandmother's heirloom recipe.

  • Ube Fried Brioche

    Fried brioche dough with ube, the Filipino purple yam.

  • Ube custard doughnut

    Filled with ube custard, one of the bakes Kora opened with.

  • Apple Fritter

    A classic that turns up again and again in reviews.

Babka and laminated

  • Salted Egg Ube Babka

    Ube babka with a salted egg twist.

  • Croissants

    Flaky croissants share the case with the doughnuts.

Savory

  • Scallion pancake croissant

    A savory crossover, for the not-so-sweet tooth.

Seasonal

  • Ube ice cream sandwich

    A summer menu appearance.

No prices listed here: they change with the case. Popular bakes sell out, so check @fromkora for the day's lineup.
