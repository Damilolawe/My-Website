Our story

Named for Corazon.

Kora takes its name from co-founder Kimberly Camara's grandmother, Corazon. Her recipe book was the starting point for the first doughnuts, and her flan still tops the best-seller.

  1. 2020

    A Woodside apartment kitchen

    Kimberly Camara and Kevin Borja were both laid off from hospitality jobs at the start of the pandemic, and began frying doughnuts at home.

  2. Pop-ups

    Commissary kitchens and pick-up days

    Demand outgrew the apartment. Kora moved into commissary kitchens and ran pick-ups at Alewife Brewery.

  3. 10,000

    A waitlist the size of a neighborhood

    Over four years of pop-ups, the waitlist for a box reached about 10,000 people.

  4. Mar 2025

    A permanent home in Sunnyside

    The first brick-and-mortar bakery opened at 45-12 Greenpoint Avenue. Hundreds lined up for the soft opening, and the case sold out within hours.

  5. Jul 2025

    Queens' Best Bakery

    Four months in, Kora won the inaugural Queens' Best Bakery competition from the Queens Chamber of Commerce.
