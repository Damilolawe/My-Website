03 / Life outside class

Movement brings us together.

PETALED is part of the Heights, not simply another place to sweat.

Community first

The good stuff is shared.

Founder Kourtney Patterson has extended the studio's reach through community walks such as Saturday Strides, gatherings that support local businesses, and service projects including school supplies and food donation drives.

That spirit of connection is part of the studio's identity: people moving, meeting and building confidence together. Live event dates should come from the studio's own channels rather than being invented for this concept.
