01 / The story

A studio that grows with you.

For founder Kourtney Patterson, progress isn't about perfect form on day one; it's about finding your strength together.

A new beginning in the Heights

Movement, with meaning.

After years teaching Zumba, spin, and bootcamp, Kourtney Patterson built PETALED to create a more personal approach to fitness. The Jersey City Heights studio opened in 2026 with an emphasis on strength, confidence, and community.

Small-group Springboard Pilates sits at the center of the offering. The focus is on controlled movement and lasting alignment, while the atmosphere stays welcoming to different ages, abilities, and experience levels.
