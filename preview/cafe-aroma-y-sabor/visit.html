Good to know

Is it a sit-down cafe?

There is a seat if you want one, but Aroma y Sabor is built for the quick, friendly stop on your way through Sunset Park.

Do you have Latin coffee classics?

Cafe con leche, cortaditos and an iced horchata latte anchor the menu alongside the espresso bar.

Is there food?

A pastry case with conchas, empanadas and tres leches, plus a breakfast sandwich.

How close is the train?

One minute from the 36 Street station — grab-and-go friendly.
