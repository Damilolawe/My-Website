Who Is

Roxxy Haze

Comedian, writer, actor & pop culture polymath — Roxxy Haze doesn't pick a lane, she builds the whole highway.

From the mic to the manuscript, the stage to the stream, Roxxy moves fluently across comedy, writing, acting and music. It's all one language to her: the language of pop culture, spoken loud and spoken often.

A lifelong anime and gaming obsessive, Roxxy pours that fandom straight into the work — whether she's cracking jokes, writing pages, dropping tracks, or going live for the chat. Half famous, fully committed.
