Late hours

2 PMto2 AM

Baby Cafe’s Instagram bio lists the cafe as open every day from 2 in the afternoon until 2 in the morning, which makes it one of the neighborhood’s after-dark stops for fuchka and cha-time snacks.

Hours can change around holidays and Ramadan. Check @babycafe.ny before you head over.
