Good to know

What is Bao Dumpling?

A fast-casual dumpling house that debuted at 40 Gold Street on October 1, 2026 — sauce-tossed dumpling bowls alongside bao, rice bowls, noodles and shareable bites.

Can I order for delivery?

Yes — order online through Uber Eats for pickup or delivery, or stop in to dine or take out.

What should I get first?

Start with a signature dumpling bowl — the Spicy Kung Pao Chicken or Orange Beef — then add bao or a side.

Where exactly is it?

At 40 Gold Street, New York, NY 10038, in the Financial District.
