The Gold Street debut

A new dumpling stop built for downtown pace.

Bao Dumpling debuted at 40 Gold Street on October 1, 2026, as part of a three-concept fast-casual destination with Kyuramen Express and Aokō Matcha.

Its focus is direct: sauce-tossed dumpling bowls alongside rice bowls, bao, noodles and shareable bites for dine-in, takeout or delivery.

One address, three concepts

Build the meal you want.

Bao Dumpling shares 40 Gold Street with Kyuramen Express and Aokō Matcha, bringing dumplings, ramen, drinks and dessert together under one roof.
