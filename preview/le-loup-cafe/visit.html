Good to know

When is pastry freshest?

Viennoiserie comes out through the morning — the earlier you come, the fuller the case.

Can I take a cake to go?

Yes. The dessert case is built for both staying and taking away; whole cakes can be ordered ahead.

Do you have coffee?

Espresso, cappuccino and filter coffee, poured all day alongside the bakery.

Where exactly are you?

On Steinway Street in Astoria, Queens — at 22-80 Steinway Street, NY 11105.
