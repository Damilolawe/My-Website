01 / The people

Born from a love of home.

The story of Om Wok is also a story about leaving home, building a community, and keeping a food tradition alive.

People before plates

A new beginning in Queens.

Om Wok's story is connected to Sonam Yarpel, a Tibetan refugee who built a reputation for momo dumplings in Jackson Heights. In the wider Om food family, Sonam's partner Kunsang Choephel is an Army veteran and Tibetan refugee who describes food as a way to reconnect with home.

Their publicly shared story brings a human perspective to Queens' Himalayan food scene. This independent concept honors that background without claiming that the Astoria location has the same team, service, or menu as other locations.
