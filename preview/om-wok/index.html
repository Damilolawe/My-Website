01 / The experience

Made with memory. Shared with everyone.

Om Wok comes from a Tibetan culinary story shaped by Sonam Yarpel and the larger Om food family. Its established Jackson Heights restaurant is known for momo dumplings and Tibetan comfort dishes.

A new listing at 33-09 36th Avenue brings the name to Astoria. This concept explores the heritage without assuming that both locations have identical menus, hours, or service. Meet the people behind Om Wok
