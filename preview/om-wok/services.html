02 / Food & culture

The dumpling has a story.

Om Wok's established Jackson Heights menu spans Tibetan favorites and cross-Asian comfort food.

A signature tradition

Momos, in every mood.

Om Wok's Jackson Heights menu lists steamed and fried momo, including beef, chicken, potato and chive varieties, as well as a chili-sauced version called C-Momo.

The dumplings are part of Himalayan food culture. They are mentioned here to describe the established brand, not to claim they are currently available at the Astoria address.
