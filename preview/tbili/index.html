What’s on the table.

  1. Shoti breadThe traditional canoe-shaped Georgian loaf, baked fresh and best torn warm by hand.
  2. KhachapuriCheese-filled bread — the heart of the Georgian table and the anchor of the daytime menu.
  3. ChikhirtmaA delicate, golden Georgian chicken soup; comfort in a bowl.
  4. AjapsandaliA spiced eggplant and vegetable stew, slow-cooked and deeply savory.
  5. Pastries & sandwichesA rotating case to go with your morning or afternoon.
  6. CoffeeEspresso and drip, poured for the kind of conversation that runs long.

Dishes reflect verified public coverage of Tbili. Prices and daily specials are intentionally left off this independent study.
