Tbili opened in October 2026 on the Williamsburg and East Williamsburg border. Mornings start with bread and coffee; afternoons slow into soups, stews and a plate or two meant to be passed around.

From the oven & kitchen

A taste of the table.

  1. Shoti breadThe traditional canoe-shaped Georgian loaf, baked fresh and best torn warm by hand.
  2. KhachapuriCheese-filled bread — the heart of the Georgian table and the anchor of the daytime menu.
  3. ChikhirtmaA delicate, golden Georgian chicken soup; comfort in a bowl.
  4. CoffeeEspresso and drip, poured for the kind of conversation that runs long.
