01 — The table

A cafe built around the Georgian supra — the shared table.

In Georgia, hospitality is a craft. Tbili brings that warmth to the Williamsburg and East Williamsburg border: bread pulled fresh from the oven, dishes meant to be passed around, and a room made for lingering over coffee with a friend.

02 — From the oven & kitchen

Baked today, served slow.

  • Shoti bread

    The traditional canoe-shaped Georgian loaf, baked fresh and best torn warm by hand.

  • Khachapuri

    Cheese-filled bread, the heart of the Georgian table and the anchor of the daytime menu.

  • Chikhirtma

    A delicate, golden Georgian chicken soup — comfort in a bowl.

  • Ajapsandali

    A spiced eggplant and vegetable stew, slow-cooked and deeply savory.

  • Pastries & sandwiches

    A rotating case of pastries and sandwiches to go with your morning or afternoon.

  • Coffee

    Espresso and drip, poured for the kind of conversation that runs long.

03 — Our rhythm

New to Lorimer Street.

Tbili opened in October 2026, a small neighborhood cafe named for the warmth at the center of Georgian cooking. Mornings start with bread and coffee; afternoons slow into soups, stews and a shared plate or two.
