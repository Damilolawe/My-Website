Good to know

What is a supra?

A supra is the traditional Georgian feast — a shared table where dishes are passed and the meal runs long. Tbili is built around that spirit.

What should I order first?

Start with warm shoti bread and a khachapuri; add a soup or stew if you are settling in for the afternoon.

Is it good for mornings?

Yes — mornings are for bread and coffee, with pastries from the rotating case.

When did Tbili open?

Tbili opened in October 2026 on the Williamsburg and East Williamsburg border.
