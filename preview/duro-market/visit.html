Good to know

Do you cater?

Platters, buffet trays and sandwich boxes are made to order for offices and gatherings — ask at the counter.

Is the buffet available all day?

The hot buffet runs through lunch and refreshes for the evening rush.

Any vegetarian options?

Build-your-own salads, eggplant parm, falafel and rotating vegetable sides keep it meat-free friendly.

Can I call ahead?

Call ahead for large orders and we will have them boxed and ready for pickup.

See you at the counter.

Good food. Hard working.
