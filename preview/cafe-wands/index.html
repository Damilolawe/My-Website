01 — The room

Made to be present in.

Café Wands comes from one of the co-owners of Chez Fifi — conceived as a place to read, eat, talk and connect. The details are considered, the pace is unhurried, and the room asks for your attention rather than your inbox.

  • No laptopsA room for conversation and books, not screens.
  • Coffee tastingA guided coffee tasting, available by reservation.
  • Bakery cafePastries and plates to settle in with, morning to evening.

02 — Coffee & cocktails

Two ways to linger.

Coffee anchors the day; in the evening, a short list of cocktails keeps the table talking. Here, drinks are in service of the company — not the main event.

The full menu, prices and hours live with the cafe.
