How the room works

Is there a laptop policy?

Yes — Café Wands is a no-laptop room. It is built for reading, eating and conversation rather than screens.

What is the coffee tasting?

A guided flight through the coffees we are pouring that week, available by reservation.

Who is behind it?

Café Wands comes from one of the co-owners of Chez Fifi, carrying the same care for detail into a neighborhood bakery cafe.

Can I come for an evening drink?

Of course. In the evening the bakery cafe keeps going with a short, considered list of cocktails and wine.

We saved you a seat.

Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn. No laptops, just good company.
