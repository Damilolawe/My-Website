Good to know

What is Solerum?

An elevated restaurant bringing the culinary traditions of Hawaii, the Caribbean and Asia together on one table — three currents, one place.

Who is behind it?

Restaurateurs Eoin Foyle and Darren Shore, whose downtown portfolio includes Mezcali and The Malt House, with Allswell in Sarasota.

When does it open?

Solerum is coming soon to 2 Gold Street. A final opening date and hours have not been announced; hours shown here are illustrative.

Where exactly is it?

At the base of 2 Gold Street, New York, NY 10038, in the Financial District.
