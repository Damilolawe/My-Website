HOSPITALITY FIRST

A new neighborhood destination from familiar downtown operators.

Solerum was announced by restaurateurs Eoin Foyle and Darren Shore, whose portfolio includes nearby Financial District restaurants Mezcali and The Malt House, along with Allswell in Sarasota.

The owners describe the new concept as an investment in the neighborhood and a continuation of their focus on distinctive cuisine and hospitality.
