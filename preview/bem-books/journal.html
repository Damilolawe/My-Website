03 / On the page

Every book is a doorway.

An online home for Black food writing in all its forms.

Curated around Black food

More than cookbooks.

BEM's bookseller identity is built around works by Black writers that connect cooking, food history and culture. The collection reaches well beyond recipes into fiction, nonfiction, poetry, children's books, culinary studies and memoir.

As of August 2026, customers can continue to shop BEM's selection online, including ebooks and audiobooks. Its regular physical bookshop and café hours are paused, so the digital collection is the appropriate starting point.
