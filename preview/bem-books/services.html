02 / What we do

Ideas belong around a table.

The BEM Cultural Foundation carries forward the work of convening people through Black food stories.

The new focus

Programming first.

Since August 2026, the Lewis Avenue space has shifted away from regular retail and café hours. BEM's sibling nonprofit, the BEM Cultural Foundation, leads cultural programming at the intersection of Black food, literature and community.

BEM has historically brought authors, chefs, historians and readers into conversation through talks, demonstrations and gatherings. Past guests and partners have included Kardea Brown and Michael W. Twitty.

Event dates, speakers, attendance rules and admission are not invented here. Refer to the foundation's published announcements to find upcoming opportunities.
