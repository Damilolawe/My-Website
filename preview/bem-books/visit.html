04 / Stay connected

The next chapter needs all of us.

Find BEM's Bed-Stuy cultural home, support its programming and stay close to the conversation.

Bed-Stuy / Brooklyn

373 Lewis Avenue.
Brooklyn, NY 11233.

Since August 10, 2026, BEM has paused normal bookstore and café opening hours. The space is being activated through scheduled Black culinary, literary and cultural programming by the BEM Cultural Foundation. It is not currently advertised as an everyday walk-in bookstore.

For current event access or community questions, contact getintouch@bembrooklyn.com. For updates, use BEM's website, newsletter and social channels.
