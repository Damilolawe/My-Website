03 / A new chapter since August 2026

The doors to ideas are still open.

As of August 10, BEM paused regular in-person bookselling and café hours at 373 Lewis Avenue. The space now emphasizes culinary and literary cultural programming via its sibling nonprofit, the BEM Cultural Foundation.

This is not a daily walk-in bookstore right now. Buy books online, find upcoming programs through official channels, support the work, or subscribe to the mailing list.
