01 / The experience

The neighborhood brings the flavor.

Jackson Heights is one of New York's great gathering places for Bengali and Bangladeshi food. Adda Kitchen is listed as a Bangladeshi restaurant at 37-11 73rd Street.

This independent concept celebrates the neighborhood and its food culture without inventing menu items, hours, or prices. Contact the restaurant to confirm current offerings before visiting.
