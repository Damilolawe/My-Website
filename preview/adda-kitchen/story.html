Where stories meet the table.

A new space for Bangladeshi flavor, Chinese dishes and the simple pleasure of sitting together.

September 2026 / Jackson Heights

Welcome to the adda.

Local coverage reports that Adda Kitchen, Dines & Chinese opened in late September 2026 at 37-11 73rd Street, a short walk from the center of Jackson Heights.

Among its owners is Aminul Islam Mithu, who welcomed visiting Bangladeshi journalists during the restaurant's early days. The business emphasizes a spacious, comfortable place where friends and families can share food and conversation.
