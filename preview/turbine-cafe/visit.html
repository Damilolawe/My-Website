05 / The building

From power plant to production floor.

  1. Early 1900s

    A power station is built on the Gowanus to supply Brooklyn’s transit system.

  2. 1950s

    The Boiler House is demolished after decommissioning, leaving the Turbine Hall standing.

  3. 1970s

    The Turbine Hall is decommissioned. In the years that follow, the empty shell becomes a graffiti landmark nicknamed the “Batcave.”

  4. 2023

    After a restoration and new addition by PBDW Architects and Herzog & de Meuron, it opens as Powerhouse Arts, a nonprofit fabrication center for metal, wood, ceramics, textiles and printmaking.

  5. Today

    Turbine Café & Bar serves the lobby, named for the hall at the heart of the building.
