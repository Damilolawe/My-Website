03 / The hall

Named for the Turbine Hall that anchors the building.

Powerhouse Arts occupies a power station built in the early 1900s for Brooklyn’s transit system. Decades after it went quiet, it reopened in 2023 as a nonprofit fabrication center for artists, with the café on the ground floor.
