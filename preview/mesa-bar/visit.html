Good to know

What is the concept?

A Southwestern-inspired cocktail and wine bar shaped by founder Levent Cakir’s fascination with the American West — ranch-house warmth, reimagined downtown.

Is there programming?

Yes — planned nights include Spaghetti Western screenings, vinyl selections and intimate wine tastings.

When does it open?

Mesa Bar is expected to open in 2026. The final opening date and hours have not been announced; hours shown here are illustrative.

Where exactly is it?

At 220 Front Street, New York, NY 10038, in the historic South Street Seaport.
