The experience

A ranch-house warmth, reimagined downtown.

Mesa Bar is planned as a personal neighborhood space shaped by founder Levent Cakir’s fascination with the American West.

The concept moves between casual and considered: an afternoon glass of wine, a happy-hour cocktail, or a late-night cold beer with vinyl playing.
