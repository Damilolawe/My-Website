Our story

Born from a love
of Thai tea.

01

The beginning

A shared passion became a place.

CHONG began with a simple idea: bring together people who love Thai tea and explore what it can become.

Inspired by Thailand’s flavors and familiar rituals, the team set out to make something new while staying true to what makes Thai tea special.

“A place where people who love Thai tea come together to explore what Thai tea can become.”

02

The art of the blend

Five teas. Thoughtfully balanced.

CHONG combines five different teas from Thailand, working with their individual flavors and aromas to create its distinctive house blend.

Each combination is a chance to discover something unexpected—and a balance that feels uniquely CHONG.

More than a café

Tea, art, music
and design.

CHONG sees the café as a place to connect, share ideas and discover new flavors—a community carried from Thailand to New York.
