New York / Matcha discovery

Not your usual latte bar.

Aokō Matcha debuted in New York's West Village in August 2025. Founder Isabella Pang introduced a five-shade gelato tasting experience, showing how different intensities of matcha can taste and feel.

Beyond gelato, the concept serves soft serve, parfaits, hojicha, cakes and a wide range of drinks. In 2026, a FiDi outpost opened as part of the Gold Street dining destination.
