03 / The tasting idea

Find your shade.

Aokō's five-level matcha gelato concept makes the depth of green tea an experience of its own.

Mellow to bold

A different side of matcha.

At Aokō's original shop, visitors can try a flight with five matcha intensities. The concept moves from approachable, sweeter flavors toward deeper green-tea character.

This website uses five illustrative shades as a visual motif; the colors are not a scientifically standardized strength scale or a guaranteed representation of exact Aokō formulations.
