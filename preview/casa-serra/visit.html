Good to know

What is Casa Serra?

A greenhouse-inspired Italian restaurant from the House of Pasta team — bright, planted, and designed to bring an airy sense of nature to the historic Seaport.

When does it open?

Public opening coverage expects the restaurant in winter 2026. The final opening date and hours have not been announced; hours shown here are illustrative.

What’s the cooking like?

Generous, seasonal Italian — antipasti to share, handmade pasta, and dolci to linger over under the glass.

Where exactly is it?

At 227 Front Street, New York, NY 10038, in the South Street Seaport.
