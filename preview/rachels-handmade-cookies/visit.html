Good to know

Do you take custom orders?

Yes — reach out ahead of time for celebration boxes, platters and flavors or colors to match your day.

How far ahead should I order?

A few days is plenty for most boxes; larger platters and custom work are best with about a week’s notice.

Are the cookies baked fresh?

Always — small batches, by hand, in the Rosedale kitchen.

Can I pick a mix?

Of course. Half dozens and dozens can be mixed and matched across the flavor list.
