Good to know

What is Greek frozen yogurt?

It is made with thick, strained Greek-style yogurt — tangier and creamier than ordinary froyo, and lovely with honey and nuts.

Can I build my own cup?

Yes — pick a flavor (or two) and load up at the toppings bar with fruit, honey, baklava crumble and more.

Where does the name come from?

Fira is the capital of Santorini, the white-and-blue town above the Aegean — the inspiration for the whole corner.

Where are you?

On Bedford Avenue in Williamsburg, Brooklyn — at 294 Bedford Avenue.
