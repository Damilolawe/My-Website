The name

Named for the top of the island.

Fira takes its name from the capital of Santorini — the white-and-blue town perched above the Aegean. A fitting name for a bright, cooling corner built around thick, tangy Greek frozen yogurt.

Spin it plain with honey and walnuts, or pile it high with fruit, syrups and crunch. Every cup is made to order.
