01 / Chef Chikara SonoRooted in Hokkaido

A language of seasons.

Raised in Hokkaido, Chef Chikara Sono brings decades of Japanese culinary tradition and deeply personal hospitality to New York.

After training in Japan, he arrived in New York City in 1986. His celebrated work at Kyo Ya earned a Michelin star and a three-star review from The New York Times.

Kappo Sono carries forward the chef’s passion for hyper-seasonal ingredients, craft and the human connection that develops around the counter.

02 / A living traditionBeyond technique

Precision with generosity.

Chef Sono’s cooking draws on seasonal ingredients, Japanese tradition, and the openness to bring unfamiliar expressions to the table.

For guests, the experience is both highly considered and disarmingly warm — an atmosphere made possible by the proximity between chef and diner.
