01 / The philosophySeasonal by nature

Where the moment becomes the meal.

Kaiseki honors the passing of time. Each course takes shape around what nature offers now, considered and prepared with quiet intention.

The essence of kappo lies in connection: between chef and guest, ingredient and season, precision and spontaneity.
