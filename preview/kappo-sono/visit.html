02 / Good to knowBefore your visit

Questions, answered.

Where is the new Kappo Sono location?

135 West 42nd Street, New York, NY 10036, at One Bryant Park. This address is posted on the restaurant’s official website.

When does the restaurant reopen?

The official website announces a grand opening on November 18, 2026. Check directly with the restaurant for any scheduling changes.

How do I make a reservation?

Review the restaurant’s existing Resy listing . The linked listing currently displays the former East 13th Street address and historical terms. Verify the new venue before placing a deposit.

Is the menu on this site the current one?

No. The menu shown here is an explicitly dated July 2024 archive, presented to illustrate the style and structure of Kappo Sono’s kaiseki. The November 2026 menu is not yet verified.

What about pricing, seating times and dress code?

The restaurant’s prior listings described specific seating times, prices and dress expectations. Those details have not been confirmed for the new Midtown location; consult Kappo Sono directly before booking.
