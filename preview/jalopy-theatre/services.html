02 / School of Music

Learn a song. Find your people.

Jalopy's music school welcomes children and adults, first-timers and lifelong learners alike.

From the first note

Music you can take part in.

Jalopy's group classes take a relaxed, song-first approach. Students begin learning songs early rather than waiting to master every technique before playing with others.

The school offers adult classes, children's programming, workshops and private lessons, with financial aid and scholarships available. Instrument rental options are offered for some courses.
