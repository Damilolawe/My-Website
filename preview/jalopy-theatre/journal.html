Some nights become songs.

Folk, blues, roots music and traditional sounds from around the world, played live in an intimate Brooklyn room.

The stage

Hear it where it happens.

Jalopy presents performances each week with musicians from New York and beyond. The live calendar changes frequently, so this design connects directly to the official schedule rather than showing stale dates.

Alongside individual concerts, the Jalopy community supports the Brooklyn Folk Festival and musical gatherings that bring generations and traditions together.
