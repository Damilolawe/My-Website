01 / Our mission

Every song needs somewhere to live.

From stage to classroom, Jalopy keeps folk and traditional music moving between people and generations.

What we believe

Handmade. Personal. Shared.

Jalopy Theatre is a nonprofit arts organization committed to evolving folk and traditional music from New York City, the Americas and the wider world.

Here, musical heritage is not simply displayed. It is passed along through performances, workshops, classes and time spent together. Jalopy's mission embraces artists, audiences and students of all ages and abilities.
