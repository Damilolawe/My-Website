WorksbyNET is the artist-run practice of Nicholas Talamo — a human-scale studio in Bushwick where ceramics, photography, design and painting share a bench.

Material first. Small batches. A direct, personal connection to the people who end up living with the work.

01

Material

Clay, silver, ink and paper — chosen for how they age and hold a mark.

02

Making

Thrown, glazed, exposed and printed by hand, a few at a time.

03

Keeping

Finished to be used and held onto, not just looked at.

Who is behind WorksbyNET?

Nicholas Talamo, an artist working across ceramics and analog and digital photography, with graphic design and painting alongside.

Do you take commissions?

Yes — small-batch ceramics and editioned works for collectors, spaces and considered gifts. Reach out with the idea and the timeline.

Where is the studio?

In Bushwick, Brooklyn — an artist-run space founded in 2025.

How do I buy a piece?

Availability moves with each batch; the quickest way to ask about a current piece is through Instagram.
