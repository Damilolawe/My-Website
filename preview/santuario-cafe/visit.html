Good to know

Is there seating to work or read?

Yes — Santuario is built to be a quiet corner. Come in for a panela latte and stay a while.

Where do the beans come from?

Our coffee is Colombian, roasted locally by Lion’s Head Coffee.

Do you have non-coffee drinks?

Guava and classic matcha, hot chocolate and more fill out the menu for the non-coffee crowd.

Are the empanadas made fresh?

Empanadas, pandebono and almojábana come from the counter case throughout the day.
