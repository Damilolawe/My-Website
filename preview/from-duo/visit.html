Good to know

Where does the name come from?

The house began as Duo Donuts + Matcha and grew into a wider tea-and-coffee ritual — so the two founders renamed it simply From Duo.

What is the house drink?

Matcha, whisked by hand to order. It is the reason the counter exists; everything else grew up around it.

Is there seating?

A handful of seats inside for lingering, plus counter service for anyone on the move.

Do you do oat or alt milks?

Yes — most drinks can be made with the milk you prefer.

See you on 12th Street.

Two founders, one counter, matcha the slow way. Follow along for daily bakes and specials.
