01 — The name

From two people, for a whole neighborhood.

Previously announced as Duo Donuts + Matcha, the business grew beyond donuts into a wider tea-and-coffee ritual and introduced its new name: From Duo. Two founders, one counter, a neighborhood worth of regulars.
