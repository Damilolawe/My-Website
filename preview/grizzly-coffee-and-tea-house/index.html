Five ways to take your chai

Order a cup for yourself, or a pot for the table. These are the chais customers mention most on Grizzly's listings and reviews.

  1. 01

    Kashmiri chai

    The pink one: a Kashmiri-style tea, often ordered by the pot.

  2. 02

    Masala chai

    Spiced milk tea, the one reviewers recommend first.

  3. 03

    Karak chai

    Strong, milky and brewed hard.

  4. 04

    Doodh patti

    Tea leaves brewed straight in milk.

  5. 05

    Gur wali chai

    Chai sweetened with gur, unrefined cane jaggery.
