Player bio

Three decades on screen.
Thousands of hours on stream.

Khaleed "Khleo" Thomas broke out as a child actor — unforgettable as Hector "Zero" Zeroni in Holes — and kept working across film and TV, from Roll Bounce to Walking Tall and beyond.

Today he's a full-time creator: streaming games daily on Twitch, hosting the How To Nerd podcast, running the 4Kontrollers gaming crew, making music, and building one of the warmest communities online.
