Who is

Tony Baker

Chicago native, stand-up comedian and actor Tony Baker has been making people laugh for nearly a decade — blessing stages around the world with his own brand of humor and style.

Beyond the hottest comedy clubs in the country, Tony organically made millions laugh worldwide with his legendary viral voiceover videos. That comedic skill carried straight to the big screen and television.

Affectionately called "your favorite comedian's favorite comedian," Tony's comedy has become a movement — and his fans can prove it by quoting a few of his famous Bakerisms. So go ahead: get in on this, and feel every piece of the laughter.
