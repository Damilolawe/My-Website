Who is

Tony Baker

Chicago native, stand-up comedian and actor Tony Baker has spent more than a decade making audiences laugh — blessing stages around the world with his own brand of humor and style.

From packed comedy clubs to television appearances and stand-up specials, Tony has developed an unmistakable comedic style built around timing, storytelling, and personality.

Affectionately called "your favorite comedian's favorite comedian," Tony continues to connect with audiences through live performances, memorable characters, and comedy that feels uniquely his own.
