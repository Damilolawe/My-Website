01 — Who

Kevin
Fredericks

ActorComedianSpeakerDirectorFilmmakerContent CreatorInfluencer

KevOnStage built his name making the internet laugh — then built an entire business around it. From sold-out theaters and comedy clubs to a streaming app of his own, he's part comedian, part entrepreneur, part full-service comedy flywheel.

He headlined Bald Brother alongside Tony Baker (now streaming on Tubi), hosts the Here's The Thing podcast, teaches creators through his Masterclass, and runs KevOnStage Studios — a home for his own shows and a new generation of talent.
