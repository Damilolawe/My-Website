About

Behind the lens.

Joshy Gonz is a photographer and visual storyteller — chasing light, moments and the people in front of his lens, and turning them into images worth keeping.

His work pairs a photographer's patience with a storyteller's eye, carrying across his stills, his vlog and his podcast — and into the causes he shows up for.
