About

Behind the lens.

Joshy Gonz is a photographer and visual storyteller — chasing light, moments and the people in front of his lens, and turning them into images worth keeping.

From concert stages to intimate portraits, his images preserve the atmosphere of the moment. Beyond photography, Joshy brings the same point of view to conversations on Here's the Thing.
