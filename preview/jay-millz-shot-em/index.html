04 / GOOD TO KNOW

BEFORE
THE FLASH.

Booking details are set by the photographer. Always review the full current terms when selecting your appointment.

HOW DO DEPOSITS WORK?

The Quick Lifestyle, Lifestyle, and Studio packages list a 50% non-refundable deposit, applied toward the full session cost. Check the scheduler for terms specific to any other package.

WHEN WILL I RECEIVE MY IMAGES?

The Quick Lifestyle, Lifestyle, and Studio sessions list a 3–4 day turnaround and include all raw images and five edited images. Delivery conditions may vary for other packages.

CAN I BRING SOMEONE WITH ME?

Quick Lifestyle and Lifestyle sessions list a $50 additional-person fee. Studio is listed as a one-person session. Check details for the package you choose.

CAN JAY TRAVEL FOR A SHOOT?

Yes. His scheduler lists travel, vacation, and fly-out offerings. For travel availability, Jay requests a direct Instagram message with your destination, dates, group size, and shoot details.
