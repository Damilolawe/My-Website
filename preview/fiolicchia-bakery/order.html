Good to know

Where do I pick up?

In Astoria on your chosen bake day. The exact pickup details arrive with your order confirmation from the official order page.

Do you deliver?

Local delivery within Astoria is available for most orders — just select it at checkout where offered.

How far ahead should I order a cake?

Layered cakes and tarts are made to order, so a few days’ notice is best for celebrations.

What makes the focaccia different?

A biga pre-ferment, 80% hydration and a full 48-hour cold rise, all on organic flour and Italian extra virgin olive oil.
