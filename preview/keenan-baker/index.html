Biography

The thoughts people don't realize they share — until he says them out loud.

Keenan Baker is a stand-up comedian known for sharp observational humor, emotional intelligence, and a calm confidence that makes audiences feel instantly comfortable.

His comedy focuses on real-life experiences, human behavior, and the thoughts people don't realize they share until he says them out loud. Touring nationally and performing in clubs, theaters, and special events, Keenan has built a reputation for comedy that feels like a conversation — approachable, honest, and consistently funny.

His work resonates with audiences looking for humor that's smart, edgy, relatable, and rooted in genuine connection.
