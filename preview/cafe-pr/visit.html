Good to know

Do you take reservations?

Café Pri is a walk-in neighborhood spot — grab a table when you arrive. Weekend mornings can get busy.

Is it kid and family friendly?

Very. The silogs and sweets are a hit with the whole table, and there is always something familiar on the menu.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes — pancit, vegetable lumpia and the full coffee-and-pastry menu are meat-free.

Can I order coffee to go?

Always. The dalgona and ube latte travel just fine.
