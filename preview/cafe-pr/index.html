02Our story

Brooklyn warmth, Queens chapter.

Pauline Coronado and her husband Ryan brought Café Pri’s comforting coffee and Filipino-American fusion cooking from Brooklyn to Middle Village in 2025 — a neighborhood table rooted in family and hospitality. Breakfast runs long here; so do the conversations.
